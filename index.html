About me

I’m Levi Ledic, a student from Wisconsin who enjoys creating things that combine technical problem-solving with clean, thoughtful design.

My projects span robotics, web development, ESP32 hardware, automation, smart-home systems, 3D printing, physical prototypes, and experimental software. I like taking an idea, understanding how its parts work, and developing it into something useful.

Outside of technology, soccer is one of my main interests. It has helped me develop teamwork, awareness, discipline, quick decision-making, and the ability to keep improving through repetition.
