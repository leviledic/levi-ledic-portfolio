Hobbies & Interests

Things I like spending time on, learning from, and building around.

This page is a simple look at the interests that shape what I build and what I keep learning.

Some of these are hobbies. Some are project directions. Some are things I keep returning to because they feel useful, hands-on, creative, or worth improving.

01

Soccer

Soccer is one of my personal interests because it involves timing, movement, teamwork, and quick decisions.

02

Design

I like design when it feels simple, calm, and easy to understand without needing extra explanation.

03

Building

I like taking ideas and making them real, whether that means a page, a tool, a setup, a prototype, or something physical.

04

Smart Spaces

I'm interested in smart spaces because they connect real rooms, controls, sensors, dashboards, and everyday routines.

05

Robotics

I participated on a VEX Robotics team, competed at the state level, and was part of a team that won an official VEX award.

06

Hands-On Projects

I like hands-on projects because they include 3D printing, physical builds, prototypes, testing, and improving ideas over time.

07

Web Ideas

I like web ideas that start simple but can turn into useful pages, tools, dashboards, or interactive experiences.

08

Learning

I have participated in multiple educational USM classes and courses, and I like learning through both structured classes and trying things myself.
